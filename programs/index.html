Our Programs

MACONA operates seasonal campaigns across Africa and the Greater Baltimore Area, focused on clothing, food, education, and medicine.

Clothing & Supply Drives

We run seasonal clothing and supply drives at local high schools, tech events, and the Microsoft campus. Our 2024 H1 drive collected 693 items toward our goal of 1,000 — clothing, shoes, books, and stuffed animals for children in need.

International Shipments to West Africa

Every donation is shipped directly to communities in need. We send tracked container shipments from Maryland to Dakar, Senegal — GPS-tracked the entire journey across the Atlantic. Our shipment trackers (MACONA-TKR-1 through TKR-4) ensure full transparency.

How We Operate

Our strategy relies on Cash and In-Kind donations. Cash drives run in H1 and H2 semesters. In-Kind drives (clothing, food, educational supplies, medicine) are seasonal — Spring, Summer, Fall, and Winter.

  • Cash donations through macona.org, Givebutter, and Benevity corporate matching
  • In-Kind donations collected at local events, schools, and corporate campuses
  • International shipping tracked via GPS from collection to delivery

Shipment Tracking

We track every shipment from Maryland to West Africa. Our GPS trackers (MACONA-TKR-1 through TKR-4) provide real-time visibility as supplies cross the Atlantic to Dakar, Senegal.
