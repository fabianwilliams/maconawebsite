Our Programs

MACONA operates programs across West Africa focused on three pillars: education, clean water, and community development.

🎓

School Supplies Distribution

Distributing learning materials — textbooks, notebooks, pens, and educational supplies — to students in underserved communities across West Africa.

Clean Water Access

Providing access to safe, clean drinking water through well construction, water purification systems, and community education on water safety and sanitation.

Improving health outcomes for entire communities

Community Development

Building local capacity through teacher training programs, youth mentorship, infrastructure improvements, and sustainable economic development initiatives.

Summer Youth Initiative

Our flagship education program serves 200 youth in West Africa through summer tutoring, school supply distribution, and training for 20 local teachers to build lasting capacity. By investing in both students and educators, we create a multiplier effect that extends far beyond the summer months.

How We Deliver

MACONA partners with local organizations in West Africa to ensure programs are culturally appropriate, efficiently delivered, and create lasting impact. Our all-volunteer model means 100% of program funding goes directly to the communities we serve.
