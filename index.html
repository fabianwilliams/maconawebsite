What We Do

Our Programs

Focused initiatives that create lasting change in the communities we serve.

Clothing & Supply Drives

We collect clothing, school supplies, food, and medicine through seasonal drives at local high schools, tech events, and the Microsoft campus. 693 items collected toward our 1,000 goal in 2024.

Learn more →

International Shipments

Tracked container shipments deliver donated supplies directly to communities in Dakar, Senegal and across West Africa. Every shipment is GPS-tracked from Maryland to Africa.

Learn more →

Community & Education

Healthcare, education, and nutrition programs for children and women in Africa and the Greater Baltimore Area. Sustainable solutions in partnership with local communities.
