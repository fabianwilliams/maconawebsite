Meet Mimi — Our AI Executive Assistant

As a 100% volunteer organization, we needed to find creative ways to multiply our impact. Mimi is our AI executive assistant that handles communications, scheduling, and operational coordination — freeing our volunteers to focus on what matters most: the communities we serve.

From drafting donor updates to coordinating supply chain logistics, Mimi helps a small nonprofit operate with the efficiency of an organization ten times our size.
