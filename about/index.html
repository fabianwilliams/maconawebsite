Our Mission

Empowering Communities Through Direct Action

MACONA is dedicated to providing relief and support for children and women in Africa and the Greater Baltimore Area. Our mission is to empower communities by providing access to basic needs such as healthcare, education, and nutrition. We strive to create sustainable solutions that will improve the lives of those in need, and to work towards a future where poverty and inequality are no longer barriers to a fulfilling life. Our efforts will be focused on Africa and the greater Baltimore area, and we will work in partnership with local communities to achieve our goals.

Our Vision

A Future Without Barriers

To create a world where every child and woman has access to basic needs such as healthcare, education, and nutrition. We believe that every individual deserves the opportunity to live a healthy and fulfilling life, regardless of their circumstances.

Campaigns & Operations

How We Raise and Deploy Resources

MACONA's strategy relies on both Cash and In-Kind donations, organized around seasonal campaigns and semester-based fundraising cycles.

Seasonal Drives

We run four seasonal campaigns each year — Spring, Summer, Fall, and Winter — to collect clothing, food, educational supplies, and medicine for communities in need.

Cash Fundraising

Cash drives are organized into two semesters: H1 (January-June) and H2 (July-December). Funds are raised through our website, Givebutter for direct cash donations, and Benevity for corporate matching programs.

In-Kind Donations

We collect clothing, food, educational supplies, and medicine through drives at local high schools, tech events, and the Microsoft campus, as well as local food and clothing drives.

International Shipments

Collected goods are tracked and shipped internationally, with shipments to Dakar, Senegal among our established routes for reaching communities in Africa.

On the Ground

Where We Show Up

Local High Schools

Clothing drives organized at high schools in the Greater Baltimore Area, engaging students and families in direct community action.

Tech Events

Clothing and supply drives hosted at technology conferences, connecting the tech community with hands-on philanthropy.

Microsoft Campus

Clothing drives on the Microsoft campus, leveraging employee giving programs and corporate matching through Benevity.

Local Community Drives

Food and clothing drives organized locally in the Laurel, Maryland area and Greater Baltimore region.
