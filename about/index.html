About MACONA

A 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization building a brighter future for communities in West Africa through education, clean water, and sustainable development.

Our Mission

Transforming Communities Through Direct Action

MACONA (Mimi African Charities Organization of North America) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to improving the lives of communities in West Africa through education, clean water, and sustainable development.

Founded by Mariam Williams, MACONA operates on a simple belief: every child deserves access to education, every family deserves clean water, and every community deserves the tools to build a sustainable future.

Our Story

From Personal Connection to Community Impact

MACONA grew out of a deep personal connection to West Africa and a commitment to making a tangible difference. Our founder, Mariam Williams, saw firsthand the challenges facing communities — under-resourced schools, limited access to clean water, and a lack of sustainable infrastructure — and knew that meaningful change was possible with the right approach.

Rather than building a large bureaucratic organization, Mariam chose a different path: an all-volunteer model where every dollar donated goes directly to programs on the ground. There is no paid staff. No overhead salaries. No expensive office space. Just a dedicated team of volunteers who share a vision of empowered communities.

This lean approach means donors can trust that their contributions create real impact — funding school supplies, training teachers, and delivering clean water to families who need it most.

Mariam Williams

CEO & Founder

Mariam founded MACONA with a vision of creating direct, lasting impact in West African communities. Drawing on her personal connection to the region and her professional experience, she built an organization that prioritizes efficiency and transparency. Under her leadership, MACONA has grown from a grassroots effort into a recognized 501(c)(3) nonprofit with technology-forward operations and strong community partnerships.

MACONA is governed by an all-volunteer board of directors who share Mariam's commitment to transparency, direct impact, and responsible stewardship of donor resources.

How We Work

The All-Volunteer Model

MACONA's strength is our lean, volunteer-driven structure. By eliminating administrative overhead, we ensure that donations go where they matter most — directly to the communities we serve.

Local Partnerships

We partner with trusted organizations already embedded in West African communities. These local partners understand the needs on the ground and ensure that resources reach the right people efficiently.

School Supplies & Education

We distribute school supplies and learning materials to under-resourced schools, removing barriers that keep children from accessing education. Our programs have served over 200 students to date.

Teacher Training

We invest in educators through hands-on training programs that equip local teachers with modern teaching methods. Twenty teachers across our partner schools have completed our training programs.

Summer Youth Initiative

Our signature Summer Youth Initiative brings together students for intensive learning experiences that go beyond the traditional school year — building skills, confidence, and community among young learners.

Innovation

Small Organization, Enterprise Technology

We believe small organizations deserve the same technology advantages as large enterprises.

MACONA is one of the first nonprofits to deploy an AI executive assistant — Mimi — to handle daily operations. From social media management to donor research and communications, Mimi automates the work that would normally require dedicated staff, so our CEO can focus on strategy and relationships.
