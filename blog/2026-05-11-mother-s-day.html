Mother's Day: Empowering Mothers with MACONA

Explore how MACONA supports maternal health and the women it serves on this special Mother's Day.

Why This Day Matters to MACONA

At MACONA, we strive to empower women, particularly mothers, by providing essential resources that have a direct impact on their daily lives. Mother's Day is a reminder of the strength and dedication mothers exhibit every day without recognition.

Our projects, including the distribution of hygiene kits and nutritious food bags, aim to ease the burden on mothers who must provide with limited resources. On our last campaign, we recorded significant attendance improvements in areas where maternal support was effectively delivered.

One Mother's Story

The mothers MACONA serves are the ones holding their families together — keeping children fed, walking to the well at dawn, signing up for literacy classes after long days, weaving daily resilience into the fabric of community life. This Mother's Day, we honor them, and the donors and volunteers whose continued support makes our programs possible.

The Work / The Pipeline

From sourcing supplies to the effective distribution and follow-up, MACONA's work guarantees accountability and measurable outcomes. Our team ensures that items, including school supplies and food, reach the intended beneficiaries promptly and safely, reinforcing our mission of transparency.
