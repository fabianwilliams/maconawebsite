2024 Clothing Drive — 693 Items and Counting

Help us reach our goal of 1,000

Our 2024 H1 drive has collected 693 clothing items toward our goal of 1,000 — that’s 69% of our target! Every item collected goes directly to communities in West Africa who need it most.

Clothing collected during the 2024 drive — shirts, pants, shoes, dresses, and children’s clothes ready for shipment.

Beyond T-Shirts

This year, we’ve expanded beyond T-shirts to accept any article of clothing — shirts, pants, shoes, dresses, and children’s clothes. The need is broad, and we want to make it as easy as possible for people to contribute what they have.

Where We Collect

Drives run at local high schools, tech events, and the Microsoft campus. We set up collection bins, spread the word, and our volunteers sort and pack everything for shipment. If you’re at any of these locations, look for the MACONA collection bins.

Where It Goes

All collected items are shipped to communities in West Africa via our tracked container shipments. We know where every container goes, and we follow up with receiving communities to confirm delivery. This isn’t a drop-it-and-forget-it operation — we track the full chain from collection to distribution.
