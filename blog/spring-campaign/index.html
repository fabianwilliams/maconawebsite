Spring 2024

Spring Giving Campaign 2024

Join us in making a difference this spring

Spring is a season of renewal and growth, and MACONA’s annual spring campaign is our biggest fundraising moment of the year. This is the time when the generosity of our supporters translates directly into impact — school supplies shipped, teachers trained, and clean water delivered to communities in West Africa.

This Year’s Priorities

Every dollar raised during the spring campaign goes directly to three priorities:

  • School supply distribution for the upcoming academic year — textbooks, notebooks, pens, and educational materials for students who would otherwise go without
  • Teacher training expansion to reach more communities, building the local capacity that sustains learning long after our supplies arrive
  • Clean water infrastructure improvements — well construction and water purification systems that improve health outcomes for entire villages

Your Dollar at Work

$25
Supplies 5 students with a full set of school materials
$50
Contributes to clean water infrastructure for a community
$100
Funds teacher training for a local educator

Employer Matching Through Benevity

Many employers match charitable donations through Benevity. If your company participates, search for MACONA in your company’s giving portal to double the impact of your contribution. A $50 donation becomes $100. A $100 donation becomes $200. It’s one of the easiest ways to multiply your generosity.

  • Volunteer your skills — we’re always looking for talented people to support our programs
