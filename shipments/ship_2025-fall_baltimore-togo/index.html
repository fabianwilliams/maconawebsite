Completed Shipment

Philadelphia and Baltimore to Togo, Fall 2025

4 GPS trackers · packed Sep 15, 2025 · Port of Baltimore Oct 1 · Le Havre France Oct 8-10 · Lome Togo Nov 11

Delivered. Container reached Lome, Togo on November 11, 2025. Trackers then split for onward distribution across Togo, Mali, and France.
Journey Stages

The full journey, end to end

Bags packed in Maryland on September 15 with all four trackers attached. Handed off at the Philly warehouse on September 17, transferred to the Port of Baltimore on October 1. The container pinged from Le Havre, France on October 8 and again on October 10. Mid-Atlantic distance ping on October 20. By November 11, the container had reached Lome, Togo. Trackers then split. Some stayed in Togo for distribution, others continued to Mali, one surfaced back in France.

All 4 trackers active inside the Philly warehouse before container loading.

Container moves Philly to Baltimore

All 4 trackers pinging strong while the container was moved from the Philly warehouse to the Port of Baltimore.

2 of 4 trackers pinged from Le Havre on Oct 8. By Oct 10, 3 of 4 were showing in the same port.

First African landfall. 2 trackers showed at Lome port that morning; by the end of the day, 3 of 4 were pinging from the same port.

Trackers split for onward distribution

Same day, trackers split paths. 1 reading from France (likely a returning traveler), 1 in Mali, 1 still in Togo. Container contents distributed across multiple endpoints.
