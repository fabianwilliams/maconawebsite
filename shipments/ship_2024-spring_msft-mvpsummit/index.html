Completed Shipment

MVP Summit + TechCon Seattle to West Africa, Spring 2024

First MACONA shipment with a GPS tracker · collected at MSFT campus + TechCon Seattle · arrived May 2024 · distributed June 2024

Delivered. Trackers pinged from West Africa May 24, 2024. Distribution at the orphanage rolled out June 6-10, 2024.
Journey Stages

The full journey, end to end

This was MACONA's first shipment with an active GPS tracker on board. The donation collection ran through two software conferences in 2024: the Microsoft MVP Summit in March and TechCon Seattle in June. The container reached the African coast in late May, and the distribution team handed out clothing and food at the orphanage in early June.

Microsoft MVP Summit collection

Multi-day MVP Summit booth at the Microsoft campus. The donation box sat at the table all week while MVPs and Microsoft attendees dropped off clothing and supplies.

TechCon Seattle collection

The TechCon Seattle booth opened the second collection point. Conference attendees brought clothing and supplies to the MACONA table throughout the week.
